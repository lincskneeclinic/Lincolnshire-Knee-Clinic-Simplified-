## Lincolnshire Knee Clinic Digital Platform

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved SEO strategy framework  
**Primary use:** Public website, patient education and future content growth  
**Repository:** `lincskneeclinic/Lincolnshire-Knee-Clinic`

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the SEO strategy for Lincolnshire Knee Clinic.

The purpose of the SEO strategy is to make the website visible to patients searching for reliable information, assessment and treatment options for knee problems in Lincolnshire and surrounding areas.

The SEO strategy must support:

- Patient education
- Private consultation enquiries
- Injection clinic enquiries
- Condition-specific search visibility
- Treatment-specific search visibility
- Local search visibility
- Long-term content growth
- Clinically safe search optimisation

SEO must never override clinical accuracy, patient safety or content governance.

---

## 2. SEO Positioning

Lincolnshire Knee Clinic should be positioned online as:

```text
A consultant-led specialist knee clinic in Lincolnshire providing assessment, treatment information, knee injections, patient education and clear booking pathways for patients with knee pain, arthritis, sports knee injuries and knee replacement concerns.
```

The website should rank for searches related to:

- Knee pain
- Knee arthritis
- Knee replacement
- Knee injections
- Meniscus tears
- ACL injuries
- Sports knee injuries
- Private knee consultations
- Knee surgeon Lincolnshire
- Knee clinic Lincolnshire
- Knee treatment Lincolnshire

The website should not rely on exaggerated search claims such as:

```text
Best knee surgeon
Number one knee clinic
Guaranteed knee pain treatment
Pain-free treatment
```

unless claims are independently verifiable, compliant and approved.

## 3. SEO Principles

All SEO activity must follow these principles.

### 3.1 Clinical Accuracy First

Search optimisation must never introduce inaccurate or unsafe wording.

Avoid:

- Keyword stuffing
- Overpromising
- Unsupported claims
- Misleading treatment claims
- Fake local pages
- Duplicate content
- Exaggerated “best” language
- Content written only for search engines

### 3.2 Patient Search Intent

SEO should match how patients actually search.

Patients may search by:

- Symptom
- Diagnosis
- Treatment
- Injection type
- Location
- Question
- Recovery concern
- Previous surgery concern

The website should therefore support:

```text
Symptom-led pages
Condition-led pages
Treatment-led pages
Injection-led pages
Location/clinic pages
Education articles
FAQ content
```

### 3.3 One Primary Topic Per Page

Each page should have one clear primary topic.

Example:

```text
/conditions/knee-arthritis
```

should focus on knee arthritis.

It should not also try to be the main page for:

```text
knee replacement
hyaluronic acid injections
meniscus tears
ACL injuries
```

Those require their own dedicated pages.

### 3.4 Internal Linking

SEO strength should be built through logical internal links between symptoms, conditions, treatments, injections, clinics and education articles.

### 3.5 Local Relevance

The website should clearly signal its Lincolnshire relevance without creating thin or misleading location pages.

## 9. Metadata Strategy

Every page should have:

```text
Meta title
Meta description
Canonical URL
Open Graph title
Open Graph description
Page type
Clinical reviewer where relevant
Last reviewed date where relevant
```

### 9.1 Meta Title Rules

Meta titles should be:

- Specific
- Accurate
- Clear
- Under approximately 60 characters where possible
- Free from unsupported claims
- Clinically safe

Format options:

```text
[Topic] | Lincolnshire Knee Clinic
[Topic] in Lincolnshire | Lincolnshire Knee Clinic
```

Examples:

```text
Knee Arthritis Treatment | Lincolnshire Knee Clinic
Total Knee Replacement | Lincolnshire Knee Clinic
Knee Injections in Lincolnshire | Lincolnshire Knee Clinic
Meniscus Tear Treatment | Lincolnshire Knee Clinic
```

Avoid:

```text
Best Knee Surgeon Lincolnshire | Guaranteed Results
```

### 9.2 Meta Description Rules

Meta descriptions should:

- Explain the page clearly
- Match search intent
- Encourage appropriate next action
- Avoid guarantees
- Stay around 150–160 characters where possible

Examples:

```text
Learn about knee arthritis symptoms, assessment and treatment options, including physiotherapy, injections and knee replacement.
```

```text
Information about knee injections for arthritis, including corticosteroid, hyaluronic acid, PRP and Arthrosamid options.
```

```text
Find out about total knee replacement, who may benefit, risks, recovery and when specialist assessment may be appropriate.
```

## 15. Image SEO

Images should support clinical clarity and performance.

### 15.1 Image Rules

Use:

- Descriptive filenames
- Alt text where appropriate
- Compressed images
- Modern formats where suitable
- Real clinic/consultant imagery where possible

Avoid:

- Generic stock images where possible
- Misleading clinical imagery
- Decorative images without purpose

## 24. Final SEO Statement

The SEO strategy for Lincolnshire Knee Clinic is to build search visibility through clinically useful, patient-centred, consultant-led content.

The website should rank because it is clear, structured, useful, local, trustworthy and medically responsible.

It should not rank through exaggeration, keyword stuffing, unsupported claims or manipulative local SEO.

SEO should help the right patients find the right information and take the right next step.
