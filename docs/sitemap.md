**Version:** 1.0

**Primary domain:** `lincolnshirekneeclinic.co.uk`  
**Secondary domain:** `lincolnshirekneeclinic.com` redirecting to `.co.uk`  
**Repository:** `lincskneeclinic/Lincolnshire-Knee-Clinic`

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the approved sitemap and information architecture for the Lincolnshire Knee Clinic digital platform.

The platform will include:

1. A public clinical website
2. A patient education and blog system
3. A future secure patient portal
4. A future digital clinic infrastructure for appointments, documents, questionnaires, rehabilitation tracking, and communication

The sitemap must support the current public website while allowing future expansion into secure clinical workflows.

### 3.11 Urgent Advice

**URL:** `/urgent-advice`

Purpose:

Provide safety information and direct patients to appropriate urgent services.

Approved wording:

Lincolnshire Knee Clinic does not provide emergency medical care.

If you think you have a life-threatening emergency, call 999 immediately.

For urgent medical help that is not life-threatening, use NHS 111 online or call 111.

If you have severe knee pain, cannot put weight on the leg, have a badly swollen or deformed knee, or have fever, redness or heat around the knee, seek urgent medical advice via NHS 111.

If you have recently had surgery and are concerned about increasing pain, wound leakage, fever, calf swelling, chest pain or shortness of breath, contact the hospital where your surgery was performed or seek urgent medical help.

This page should be linked from:

- Utility navigation
- Footer
- Contact page
- Symptom pages
- Post-operative education pages

### 4.1 Medical Disclaimer

The medical disclaimer should make clear that:

- Website content is for general educational information only
- Website content does not replace medical consultation
- No individual diagnosis is provided through public content
- Patients should seek professional medical advice for personal clinical decisions
- Urgent symptoms require urgent medical assessment

### 4.2 Privacy Policy

The privacy policy should cover:

- What personal data is collected
- Why it is collected
- How it is stored
- How long it is retained
- Who it may be shared with
- Patient rights
- Contact details for data protection enquiries
- Use of third-party systems
- Future patient portal data handling

### 4.3 Accessibility Statement

The accessibility statement should commit the website to:

- WCAG 2.1 AA standard
- Readable typography
- Keyboard navigation
- High contrast
- Clear language
- Accessible forms
- Mobile usability
- Support for older patients

## 8. URL Rules

All URLs should be:

- Lowercase
- Readable
- Short where possible
- Hyphen-separated
- Clinically accurate
- Free of unnecessary dates unless part of article archive logic

Good examples:

- `/conditions/knee-arthritis`
- `/treatments/total-knee-replacement`
- `/injections/hyaluronic-acid-injection`
- `/education/knee-replacement/how-long-does-knee-replacement-last`

Avoid:

- `/page1`
- `/knee-stuff`
- `/best-knee-surgeon-lincolnshire-cheap-fast-treatment`
- `/blog/2026/07/09/article-123`

SEO rules:

- No duplicate clinical pages
- Use condition-specific and treatment-specific pages

- Use education articles for long-tail questions
- Use internal linking to connect symptoms, conditions and treatments
- Avoid keyword stuffing
- Prioritise clinical clarity over aggressive SEO

## 10. Content Governance

All clinical content must include:

- Named clinical author or reviewer
- Last reviewed date
- Clear medical disclaimer
- Evidence-aware wording
- No exaggerated claims
- No guaranteed outcomes
- No invented statistics
- No unsupported superiority claims

AI-generated content must follow these rules:

- AI may assist drafting
- AI must not publish directly
- Clinical review is required before publication
- Sources should be checked before clinical claims are approved
- Content must be suitable for UK patients
- Content must avoid unsafe self-diagnosis

## 11. Build Phases

### Phase 1 — Public Website Foundation

Build first:

- Home
- About
- Symptoms hub
- Conditions hub
- Treatments hub
- Injections hub
- Education & Blog hub
- Clinics
- Booking
- Contact
- Urgent Advice
- Legal and governance pages

Phase 1 booking should use:

- Microsoft Bookings
- Outlook
- Microsoft Teams where video consultations are used

Do not build a full patient portal in Phase 1.

### Phase 2 — Clinical Content Expansion

Build next:

- Full condition library
- Full treatment library
- Full injection pages
- Education article library
- FAQs
- Internal linking
- SEO metadata
- Content governance workflow

### Phase 3 — Patient Portal Design and Architecture

Build the visual and technical foundation for:

- Login
- Invite-only account activation
- Dashboard
- Appointments
- Documents
- Questionnaires
- Outcome scores
- Rehabilitation tracking

Security planning must include:

- UK GDPR
- Data Protection Act 2018
- Special category health data handling
- Multi-factor authentication
- Role-based access control
- Audit trails
- Consent
- Data retention
- Incident response

### Phase 4 — Secure Patient Portal Implementation

Implement only after governance is ready:

- Supabase authentication
- Patient records
- Document access
- Appointment data
- Outcome scores
- Rehabilitation tracking
- Secure messaging if approved
- Administrative dashboard
- Audit logs
